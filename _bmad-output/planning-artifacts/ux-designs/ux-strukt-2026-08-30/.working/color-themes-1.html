Color Themes — Discovery pass, Precise & Clinical direction. The direction (drafting-table register: hairline strokes, zero corner-radius, graph-paper canvas, monospace numerals on every engineering value) is locked. This page holds the register constant and varies only the token palette — five distinct hue/value choices, each shown in both light and dark mode, each carrying the full semantic role set so one can be lifted straight into DESIGN.md. Variation 1 reproduces the original blueprint-blue + safety-orange proposal as the baseline for comparison; the rest deliberately diverge in temperature and saturation, not just hue.
